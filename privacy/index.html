Privacy policy

This notice describes how this website handles your information.

Enquiries

The enquiry form prepares an email in your own email app. Form details are not sent to a website database. You choose whether to send the email. If you do, Ginger receives the name, email, phone number, location, project interest and message you included, and uses them to respond to your enquiry.

External services

Email, Instagram and WhatsApp links open external services. Their own privacy practices apply when you use them. The website does not include analytics scripts, advertising trackers or embedded social feeds.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website.

Your information

To ask about information you have shared, request a correction or request deletion, email Gingermarketingstudios@gmail.com. For Academy enquiries, email Gingeracademy@gmail.com.
